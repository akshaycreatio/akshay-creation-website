Good to know

Questions, answered 💡

How does listing a Creator Digital Product work?

Tap "List Your Digital Product" in the Store, enter your base price (e.g., ₹50), creator profile (YouTube/Instagram), cover photo, download link, and UPI ID. A 20% platform markup is added for buyers (e.g., ₹60 on Store), and you receive 100% of your ₹50 base price per verified sale.

How do I buy a digital pack?

Tap Buy via UPI, pay the exact amount, then submit your name, email and the 12-digit UTR. You get an Order ID and instant order summary card straight away.

How do I download after paying?

Paste your Order ID or UTR into Check in the store. When your payment is marked Paid, the download button appears.

How do Story & Post promotions work?

Community Story Promotion (₹199) and Community Post Promotion (₹299) are limited-time offers. Submit your details to get a unique token, pay via GPay/UPI with that token, and once matched and approved in the portal, your confirmation email is sent.
